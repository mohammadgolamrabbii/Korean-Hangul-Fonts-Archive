## 🌟 Prominent Typefaces & Foundations Included

1. **National Typography & Public Cultural Heritage (공공누리 / KOGL)**:
   - **KCC Heritage Series**: KCC Kimhoon (김훈체), KCC Kimhwanki (김환기체), KCC Sonkeechung (손기정체), KCC PakKyongni (박경리체), KCC Ganpan (간판체), KCC Hyerim (혜림체), KCC Jeongbeom (정범체), KCC Murukmuruk (무럭무럭체).
   - **Seoul Municipal Fonts**: Seoul Hangang (서울한강체), Seoul Namsan (서울남산체).
   - **National Education Foundation**: KERIS Baeum (한국교육학술정보원 배움체), KERIS Kedu (케듀체).
   - **Korea National Park Service**: KNPS Bandali (국립공원 반달이체), KNPS Kkomi (꼬미체).

2. **Flagship Digital Sans & Modern Workhorses**:
   - **Pretendard Family**: Standard system-ui alternative across full weights (Thin to Black).
   - **Spoqa Han Sans Neo**: High-density screen-optimized sans-serif.
   - **Wanted Sans**: Modern variable-inspired sans for web products.
   - **LINE Seed KR**: LINE Corporation's signature geometric sans.
   - **D2Coding & Monoplex KR**: Precision developer monospaced families with programming ligatures.
   - **Noto Sans & Serif CJK KR**: Pan-CJK universal workhorses.

3. **Naver Hangeul Day Cultural Preservation (네이버 한글날 손글씨 공모전)**:
   - Over 100 historical handwriting and personal calligraphy typefaces preserving individual Korean handwriting aesthetics (Nanum Brush, Nanum Pen, Nanum Gothic Eco, Nanum Myeongjo Eco, Nanum Square, Nanum Square Neo, Nanum Square Round).

4. **K-Pop, Webtoon & Entertainment Display**:
   - **CookieRun**: Devsisters' iconic high-energy game typeface.
   - **Woowa Brothers (배달의민족 / Baemin)**: DoHyeon (도현체), Euljiro (을지로체), Hanna (한나체), Jua (주아체), KirangHaerang (기랑해랑체), YeonSung (연성체).
   - **NEXON & Netmarble**: NEXON Lv1/Lv2 Gothic, Football Gothic, Netmarble Bold.
   - **Maplestory**: Nexon's legendary friendly display typeface.

5. **Retro Computing & Cyberpunk Hangul**:
   - **NeoDunggeunmo & NeoDunggeunmo Pro**: Legendary 90s DOS classic 16x16 pixel Hangul.
   - **DOS Series**: DOSGothic, DOSIyagi, DOSMyungjo, DOSPilgi, DOSSaemmul.
   - **DNF BitBit**: High-energy arcade pixel font.
